Kunduz Ai - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-07

Kunduz Ai contains no analytics and no advertising.

The app shows the owner's public links (YouTube, TikTok, TikTok

Plugin file, WhatsApp channel, WhatsApp, Facebook and Telegram).

Tapping a link opens it in the relevant app or browser on your

device; those services have their own privacy policies.

The chat

The app has an optional built-in chat. If you choose to use it, you

register with your name, email address and phone number, and that

information — together with the text, voice messages and photos you send —

is stored in Google Firebase so the chat can work and the owner can

reply to you. Your chat is private: only you and the owner can see

it. This information is never sold and never shared with anyone

else. If you never open the chat, none of this information is

collected.

The app also downloads one small public file to show the NEW badges

on the link banners; that file contains no personal data.

Contact: via the WhatsApp link or the chat inside the app.
